Something that I would deem valuable when doing social work with people in the community is the ability to communicate well with your partners. I noticed that when we were in client meetings or overall team meetings in general, everything was always conducted smoothly. This helped us be efficient with our working time and with meeting the goals of our community partners. This is honestly something that I am used to doing. Being the main person in the role of client communicator and team organizer was something that personally meshed well with my skill set. Not to say that I was the leader of our group, but being the main person to communicate and organize everyone is something that I have been doing since high school. Roles where I have been in positions that required me to communicate with others and organize teams efficiently include being a section leader, marching captain, and esports captain in high school, where much of what I did involved organizing and communicating with people. I continue to do that here at Hendrix College as the captain of our SSBU esports team, and I can proudly say that I have helped lead our team to become undefeated champions in our conference.
Through this experience, I also realized how important it is to balance communication and organization with allowing other team members to take initiative as well. During client meetings, even though I was in the primary role of client communicator, my teammates would often ask questions while I focused on taking notes throughout the meeting so we could have information to look back on later. Other team members would also schedule team meetings or update meetings to check on how the project was progressing. This experience made me reflect on how teams function effectively and how responsibilities do not have to fall on just one person. Having a team you can trust makes it easier to support one another whenever help is needed.

I absolutely feel like my community partner is accomplishing environmental change through this project. My community partner already contributes a significant amount of time and energy toward creating positive environmental change within our community. He hosts weekly gardening programs for both youth and adults who wish to learn more about gardening and sustainable practices. The seed packets that clients are allowed to check out are a great way to help people begin their gardening journeys and become more involved in growing their own plants and food.
The main reason he wanted this project was to make it easier for clients to view the organizations seed inventory and to provide them with an easy way to access information about the different types of seeds available. I believe this approach is effective because it makes gardening more accessible, especially for beginners who may not know where to start. By making the information and seeds more accessable, the project encourages more people in the community to participate in gardening.
I feel that this creates meaningful environmental change because increased participation in gardening can benefit the community in multiple ways, like promoting sustainability, encouraging healthier lifestyles, and helping people become more connected with their local environment. Overall, I believe my community partner’s approach is effective because it combines education, accessibility, and community involvement in a practical and impactful way.
