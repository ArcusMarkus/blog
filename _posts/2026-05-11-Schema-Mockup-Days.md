Hello this is a reflection post fr the Schema and Mockup Days that I am unfortunately late to posting.

Since this reflection is unforunately late in the making I will discuss about my contributions at the original time of this post. Within our team dividing work up evenly was a big goal of ours. For mockups we let each person handle one page or two pages of mockups, which everyone but the person handling the schema took at least one, while one person handled the schema. I handled the mockups for the inventory page and the checkout page. Throughout the time that our group worked together we handed off roles fairly evenly which made the workload much less of a headache for everyone. My future plans at the time were to worry about the stylistic aspects of the project as well as focusing on clear and direct communication with our clients. To me this meant handling coding cleanup, making the project fit the overall style of the library website, and getting any questions that we had answered asap.
